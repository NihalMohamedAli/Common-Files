# Rules to avoid SEO problems

1. Do not paste identical bios across all directories.
2. Use one canonical BoldSign URL per platform.
3. Add UTM tracking where allowed.
4. Use real screenshots, not generic marketing graphics.
5. Do not create fake reviews or fake likes.
6. Track every submitted and live URL.
7. Prioritize profiles where competitors already rank.
8. Refresh live profiles quarterly.
9. Pair every review profile with a customer review campaign.
10. Cross-link important integrations from the BoldSign integrations page.
